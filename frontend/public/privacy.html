Privacy Policy

Last updated:

Shelby's Arteves ("we", "us") provides AI-based operations services for local service businesses — the specific services listed on our homepage (after-hours call coverage, warranty and service recapture, reputation follow-up, lead response, quote recovery, insurance claim documentation, license and compliance tracking, and multilingual front-desk support). This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Contact form submissions: your name, business name, email address, phone number (if provided), and any message you write, when you submit our contact form.
  • Optional feedback: a 1–5 star rating and optional comment, submitted anonymously, if you choose to leave one.
  • Basic page-view analytics: which pages were viewed and the referring site, without cookies and without any information that identifies you personally.

What we don't do

We do not scrape, collect, or purchase data about you from Google Maps, social media, or any other third-party source. We do not sell, rent, or share your contact information with advertisers or data brokers. The only personal data we hold is what you directly submit to us through this site.

How we use it

Contact form submissions are used to respond to your inquiry and, if you become a customer, to deliver the services described above. We may email or call you back using the details you provide. We do not use your information for any other purpose.

How long we keep it

Contact form submissions are retained for as long as reasonably needed to respond to your inquiry and maintain business records, and are deleted on request (see Contact, below).

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or ask what we have on file, at any time by emailing shelbysarteves@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights (for example under GDPR or CCPA/CPRA); we'll honor applicable requests to the extent required by law.

Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect the information you send us, including restricting who can view submitted leads. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the business or the site changes. The "last updated" date above will reflect the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email shelbysarteves@gmail.com.
